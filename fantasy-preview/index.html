What these values mean

Above replacement = the player's period points minus the best available same-position player each week. The replacement can change each week, representing streaming. Negative values are retained. This does not include FLEX competition. Lineup impact uses the full legal nine-slot optimizer, including FLEX: your players show projected loss if removed with one same-position waiver replacement; other players show gain if added with a low-impact drop when the active roster is full. Expand a player to see the drop or replacement. These are separate counterfactuals and cannot be added to price a package.

Every player in the saved roster, sampled available-player list and forecast is listed. Players without complete forecasts or verifiable availability remain unknown. Marginal values & trade arbitrage

Compare the points a player costs their current team with the points they'd add to someone else's optimal lineup. A positive gap identifies a potential opportunity—not an acceptable trade by itself.

How the analysis works

Owner loss: lineup-point loss if the player leaves, allowing one available replacement at that position. Buyer gain: lineup-point improvement from adding him, with a low-marginal drop if at the 15-player active-roster limit. Both are what-if estimates, not literal trades.

Value gap: buyer gain minus owner loss. Your players are evaluated separately against every other roster. A complete trade cannot be priced by adding isolated values: the 1-for-1 candidates use both complete before-and-after optimized lineups. Injury-return assumptions, waiver availability and the dated model materially affect the results.

How weaknesses are identified

Weaknesses compare the opponent's optimized starting points at each slot group with the league median for that week. RB and WR each include their two required starters; FLEX is separate. The two largest deficits of at least one point are shown. Strengths use the same comparison. These are model estimates from current rosters, not verified coaching or defensive scouting reports. Unscheduled playoff opponents and missing lineups stay unknown.
