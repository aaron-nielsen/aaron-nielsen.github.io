# Historical lineup evaluation

The Transactions page shows actual Yahoo starter points and hindsight-optimal points from each week's roster. Lineup slots: QB, two RB, two WR, TE, flex, K, DEF. Points left on bench is the nonnegative difference. It is not a transaction effect.

Coverage: CSU Stats weeks 1-4, 14 teams; Nebraska week 4, one team. Missing weeks are not zero.

Historical transaction effects require complete league-wide actual player scores including unrostered players, effective transaction timestamps, and a defined policy for subsequent transactions. Pregame forecast accuracy requires immutable pregame snapshots; updated forecasts cannot substitute for past predictions.
